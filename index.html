FAQ

My cape doesn't show in-game?

Capes need Fabric or Quilt. Vanilla can't render capes offline — the launcher tells you in the log.

Black skin in catalog?

Dark skins get a subtle halo so they don't vanish on the dark card.

Emulator won't boot?

Install the Android Emulator Hypervisor Driver in SDK Manager and enable Windows Hypervisor Platform, then reboot.
