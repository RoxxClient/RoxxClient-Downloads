FAQ

My cape doesn't show in-game? — Capes need Fabric or Quilt. Vanilla can't render capes offline — the launcher tells you this in the log.

Black skin in catalog? — Dark skins are drawn with a subtle halo so they don't vanish on the dark card.

Emulator won't boot? — Install the Android Emulator Hypervisor Driver in SDK Manager and enable Windows Hypervisor Platform, then reboot.
